Fertiliser Calculation Tutor

Learn how to move from a nitrogen recommendation to fertiliser kg/ha, convert acres and hectares, and calculate total fertiliser and bags needed for a field.

Key idea

Do not confuse kg fertiliser/ha with kg N/ha

kg fertiliser/ha Actual product spread per hectare
kg N/ha Actual nitrogen supplied per hectare

For example, if urea contains 46% N and the target is 30 kg N/ha:

Fertiliser kg/ha = 30 ÷ 0.46 = 65.2 kg/ha

This app follows the conventions in the supplied teaching material. In particular, the slides use Units/acre × 2.471 = kg N/ha. For real farm use, confirm what “Units” means in the recommendation you receive.

Lesson 3 · Units/acre to kg N/ha

Follow the course convention

An adviser recommends 27 Units per acre.

kg N/ha = Units/acre × 2.471
27 × 2.471 = kg N/ha
CAN kg/ha = kg N/ha ÷ 0.275
